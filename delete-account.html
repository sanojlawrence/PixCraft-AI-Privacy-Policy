Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your account and associated data from our app, you can request deletion using the contact email below.

Steps to request deletion

  1. Send an email from your registered Google account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered email address.

Once we receive your request, we will delete your account and associated data within 7 days.

Data that will be deleted

  • Account information
  • Profile details
  • Associated user data stored in our systems

Some data may be retained temporarily if required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
